Scope: the whole Outlier web app (home, play, results, multiplayer lobby/round/podium). Mode: Experience (a game), with Operate-grade clarity for inputs and timers.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The answer is a dot; the crowd is a cloud. Rarity is drawn as distance from the crowd, so the score is a picture, not a number in a card. Refuses the quiz-app default of white cards, progress pills and confetti.
OWN-WORLD: Each genre drenches the whole screen in its own saturated field (cobalt general, moss Minecraft, plum music, violet gaming, deep teal geography, oxblood history, ochre-brown animals, tomato food, pitch-green sports, aubergine screen, indigo science). Crowd = pale dots at 30% ink; you = one hot yellow (#ffd84a) dot that never changes. Concentric tier rings in hairline ink. Type: Unbounded for numerals and the wordmark, Onest for everything else. Controls are flat ink slabs with yellow focus.
STORY: Visitor sees today's daily already waiting, plays 7 prompts in under 4 minutes, sees how far they drifted from everyone else (sigma), compares with the world, shares a row of tier squares.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed field canvas with the drifting crowd; wordmark top-left; the daily prompt card is the dominant block (Play today / your result), with Unlimited genres and Multiplayer below as a typographic list. Primary action: Play today.
FORM: code-led single-page app; seed: none (pinned) "idk be creative u can also make it diffrante depending on the ganre".
Signature interaction: on each accepted answer your dot launches out of the crowd to its tier ring, leaving a trail; your past answers stay as a constellation.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
