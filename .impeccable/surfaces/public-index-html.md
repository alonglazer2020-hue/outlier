Scope: the whole Oddity web app (home, unlimited, friends, archive, how-to, play, results, multiplayer lobby/round/final). Mode: Experience (a game) with Operate-grade clarity for the answer box and timer.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Your answer is fuel. A rarer answer burns longer, so the score is a flight you watch: a pixel rocket climbing from a dusk launch pad past real landmarks (ISS, Moon, Mars, Saturn, Voyager). Refuses the quiz-app default of cards and confetti, and refuses Krillion's cyan ocean.
OWN-WORLD: Pixel-art dusk to deep space. Ground: violet mesas and a lattice launch tower under an orange-rose horizon; sky darkens through indigo to #140b2e as altitude rises, stars fade in. Sprites are drawn in code at a coarse pixel grid (3-4 px). UI panels are night-violet slabs with cream notched pixel borders (no radius); primary buttons are sun-orange #ffb347 with dark ink. Tiers own colours: lilac grey, rose (the Copycat trap), sun, sky, violet, gold. Type: Pixelify Sans for display, HUD, buttons and prompts; Atkinson Hyperlegible for small body copy.
STORY: Open, see today's mission waiting, launch, answer 7 prompts in 25 s each, watch the rocket climb, learn which landmark you reached, compare on the board, share the coloured row.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Big pixel wordmark ODDITY at top; today's mission panel under it with LAUNCH as the primary action; four menu slabs (Unlimited, Friends, Archive, How to play); the rocket on its pad visible at the bottom (right side on desktop).
FORM: code-led single-page app; seed: none (pinned) "idk be creative whatever u want and also make the name make so sense".
Signature interaction: on every accepted answer the rocket fires and the camera climbs; passing a landmark calls it out ("Passed the Moon").
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
